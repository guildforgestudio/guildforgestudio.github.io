Cookies

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Nothing here follows you around. No advertising, no tracking pixels, no analytics company, no social media plug-ins. We keep a simple daily count of visits, which identifies nobody. This website sets no cookies at all. The web app sets two, only to keep you signed in. They are strictly necessary, so they need no consent, and that is why there is no cookie banner.

The website

  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google or any other font service.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub, which sees your IP address to deliver the page, as every web host does.
  • When a page opens, it tells our service, and we add one to that day's count: which page, roughly where you came from (such as TikTok or Discord), whether it is a phone or a computer, and the country. So as not to count you twice, your IP address and browser are scrambled with a key that exists only in memory and is thrown away every day. Neither is ever written down, and nothing links one day to the next. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, you are not counted at all.
  • A friend's referral link (?ref=CODE) is read by the page itself. When you press Open GuildForge, the code goes along in the link. When you press Download, it is copied to your clipboard so the app can offer it on its first start. This site stores nothing in your browser.

The web app

GuildForge in your browser (guildforge-q60l.onrender.com/app) is run by our service, hosted on Render. It sets two cookies, both strictly necessary and neither readable by scripts on the page:

  • gf_session keeps you signed in. It lasts 7 days, or until you sign out.
  • gf_oauth_state ties a sign-in to the browser that started it. It lasts at most 10 minutes and is removed once you are signed in.

It also keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: which server you last opened, a friend's code until you have used it, and whether you closed the invitation to our community server. Signing out removes the server choice; clearing your browser's site data removes the rest. Opening it adds one to the same daily count as above.

The desktop app

The app keeps a few things on your own computer, because it cannot work without them: your sign-in, your settings, and a note of whether it has already looked for a friend's code and how its last update went. These are strictly necessary for the app you asked for, so they need no consent. Signing out removes your sign-in; uninstalling removes the rest.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that could identify you or follow you from one day to the next, it will be off until you agree to it, and this page will say so first. Questions: guildforge2026@gmail.com.
