Cookies

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This website sets no cookies. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no social media plug-ins. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to agree to.

The website

  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google or any other font service.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub, which sees your IP address to deliver the page, as every web host does.
  • A friend's referral link (?ref=CODE) is read by the page itself. When you press Download, the code is copied to your clipboard so the app can offer it on its first start. Nothing is stored in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

The desktop app

The app keeps a few things on your own computer, because it cannot work without them: your sign-in, your settings, and a note of whether it has already looked for a friend's code and how its last update went. These are strictly necessary for the app you asked for, so they need no consent. Signing out removes your sign-in; uninstalling removes the rest.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that is not strictly necessary, it will be off until you agree to it, and this page will say so first. Questions: guildforge2026@gmail.com.
