Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This explains what GuildForge Studio collects when you use the website, the desktop app and the GuildForge bot, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do with it. The short version: we collect what the features need, we never sell it, and there are no ads and no trackers.

1. Who is responsible

GuildForge Studio is run by GuildForge Studio (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: guildforge2026@gmail.com. Full details are on the legal notice.

GuildForge is not affiliated with Discord Inc. Discord handles your Discord account under its own privacy policy.

2. What we collect and why

WhenWhatWhy, and the legal basis
You visit the website Nothing is collected by us. GitHub, which hosts the site, sees your IP address and browser to deliver the page. A friend's referral code from a link is copied to your clipboard when you press Download, and never sent to us by the website. Delivering the site (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
You sign in with Discord Your Discord user ID, username and avatar, and the list of servers you are in (Discord's identify and guilds permissions). A sign-in token, stored only as a hash, valid for 7 days. Your account, and showing which servers you can manage (contract, Art. 6(1)(b)).
Our community server With the “Join servers for you” permission you approve on Discord's sign-in screen, we add you to the GuildForge Discord server once, when you sign in. If that is not possible, we may send you at most two direct messages inviting you. You can leave at any time and we will not add you again. Support and product updates (legitimate interest; you approved it on Discord).
The bot is in a server The server's structure (channels, categories, roles and their permissions, names and topics) and the settings its admins make in GuildForge. Depending on what the admins switch on: tickets (who opened them and when), warnings, XP and levels, birthdays members enter, giveaway entries, starboard posts, reviews, security events (kept 30 days) and structure backups (kept as long as the plan allows). Running the features the server's admins chose (contract with the admin; for members, the server's and our legitimate interest in a working, safe server).
Messages in a server The bot reads messages in channels it can see, only to run the features the server switched on: filtering scams and spam, auto-responses, levels, the honeypot, logs. Messages are processed in memory and not stored by GuildForge. Message logs and ticket transcripts a server turns on are posted into that server's own channels, on Discord. Protection and moderation the server asked for (legitimate interest).
You use an AI feature What you type, and the names and topics of the server's channels and roles, are sent to our AI provider to produce the answer. We do not use them to train AI models. We record how many AI requests you make and how many tokens they used, for your plan's limits. Providing the feature you asked for (contract).
You buy a plan First and last name, email, postal code and country (as evidence of where you are, for VAT), optionally a phone number; the plan, price and payment method; your PayPal transaction ID, or your paysafecard code, which is stored encrypted and used only to redeem it. The purchase (contract) and keeping the records tax law requires (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).
You join the referral programme Your code, who used it, the commissions it earned, the PayPal address you want payouts sent to and your confirmation that you are an adult. To stop people referring their own second account, we compare account age, email address and payment method between you and the people you refer. Running the programme (contract) and preventing fraud (legitimate interest).
News from us Occasional direct messages about GuildForge updates: each announcement once, at most one a week, with a button to stop them for good. Legitimate interest; the stop button is your objection, honoured immediately.
The app crashes Nothing, unless you choose to send a crash report. You see it first; passwords, tokens and file paths are removed from it before it is even written. Fixing the problem (your consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).
Any request to our service Short-lived technical logs: the time, the request and any error. Security and fixing faults (legitimate interest).

We never sell personal data, show ads, or build profiles of you for anyone else.

3. Who else handles it

We use a few providers, each only for its job:

  • Discord: the platform the bot and the sign-in run on.
  • Render (render.com): hosts our service and its database.
  • Groq (groq.com): runs the AI models behind the AI features.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the app downloads.
  • PayPal and paysafecard: only if you pay with them, under their own terms.

Some of them are in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Sign-in tokens: 7 days. Security events: 30 days. Backups: as long as your plan keeps them.
  • A server's settings and data: until the bot is removed and you ask us to delete them.
  • Orders and payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Technical logs: a short time, then they are overwritten.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable format. Where something is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email guildforge2026@gmail.com from, or mentioning, your Discord account; we answer within one month. Records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices, are kept until that duty ends.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr); elsewhere in the EU, the one where you live.

6. Children

GuildForge runs on Discord, which is for people aged 13 or over (or older, where local law says so). It is not meant for anyone younger. Buying a plan needs you to be an adult or to have a parent's permission; referral payouts are for adults only.

7. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes, paysafecard codes are encrypted, and access to the database is limited to the service itself and its operator.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Anything important is also announced in the app and on our Discord server before it applies.
